## Auth issue and prepared fix

Earlier attempts encountered provider 429 email limits and one-time links that were invalid/expired. Auth logs confirmed both conditions. A fresh link subsequently worked; the evidence does not establish why the older links were consumed or invalid, so do not claim a specific root cause such as an email scanner.

The follow-up patch adds an explicit expired-link message, provider-throttle guidance and a 60-second resend cooldown after an accepted email request or HTTP 429 response. This improves recovery feedback; it does not raise the provider's email quota or replace SMTP configuration. Helper tests cover callback parsing and error mapping; the new UI still needs preview validation after deployment.
